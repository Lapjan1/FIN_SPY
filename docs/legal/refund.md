# Refund Policy - FIN_SPY
Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy explains trials, cancellation, and refunds for FIN_SPY page-based plans.

## Free Trial
All new accounts receive 20 free pages before any payment is required. This allows you to fully evaluate the service before committing. Your remaining pages are shown in the dashboard.

## Subscription Cancellation
You may cancel your subscription at any time from the Account page. Upon cancellation:
- Subscription remains active until end of current billing period
- No further charges will be made
- You retain access to all features until period ends
- Usage counters reset on renewal, not immediately on cancel

## Refunds
Because the service is digital and usable immediately (upload, vault storage, and extraction happen at once), we do not offer refunds for partial billing periods. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of the paid period, including any Excel workbooks, signed PDFs, and Evidence Packs already generated.

## Exceptions
If you experience a technical issue that prevents you from using the service for more than 48 consecutive hours (for example: reconciliation always failing, vault hash mismatch, extraction returning nothing, dashboard drill-down broken), contact us for a pro-rata refund or service credit. We verify such issues with our independent pack verifier and the audit hash chain.

## Dispute Resolution
If you have a billing dispute, email `admin@fin-spy.example`. We will respond within 5 business days. Please include your account ID, subscription code, and Paystack transaction reference.

## Plans
- Free: 20 pages/mo R0
- Individual: 200 pages/mo R149
- SME: 1000 pages/mo R599, 2 entities
- Practice: custom
Metering counts pages processed, AI tokens used, and exports generated, with per-account monthly caps. Estimated AI cost is tracked per document, with a global daily budget alarm and an automatic fallback to No-AI mode if the budget is exceeded.
